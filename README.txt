TRAZACORE INGENIERÍA & SISTEMAS S.A.C. — COMPANY WEBSITE (PROJECT 10)
======================================================================

HOW TO RUN LOCALLY
1. Open the TrazaCore_Website folder in Visual Studio Code.
2. Right-click index.html and select "Open with Live Server" (recommended), or open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
3. All pages share css/style.css, js/main.js, img/, media/, and downloads/.

PAGES & FEATURES
- index.html       Home (Executive standards, animated counters, Before/After comparator, video placeholder (final video to be added by owner), visual highlights)
- about.html       Corporate history, Surco & Callao visual facility showcase, 5 specialist units, and 6 Practice Directors
- industries.html  Sector filter bar + 6 photographed industry solutions cards
- services.html    6 photographed capabilities, flexible 4-stage delivery approach + executive visual roadmap, and SLA table
- technology.html  Platform Overview: interactive 4-pillar business inspector + simplified executive capability guides + link to IT Technical Brief
- projects.html    Photographed & filtered case studies (confidential terms omitted) + agnostic Industrial ROI & Efficiency Simulator
- resources.html   Executive & IT PDF library with visual previews, and 5-step Operational Readiness Check
- contact.html     Validated consultation form + corporate lounge photo + interactive Surco/Callao locator map
